Dune: Part Two

Directed by Denis Villeneuve · 2024

Sci-Fi Adventure

Dune: Part Two

2024 · Sci-Fi

Elena Rostova

Watched on March 15, 2024

star star star star star_half

Villeneuve has managed something nearly impossible here: he’s taken the dense, almost impenetrable lore of Herbert’s universe and rendered it not just coherent, but profoundly emotional. The sheer scale of Arrakis is felt in every frame, thanks to Fraser’s monolithic cinematography.

What struck me most on this viewing was the sound design. It’s oppressive when it needs to be—the thumpers echoing through the floorboards of the theater—but incredibly delicate in moments of intimacy between Paul and Chani. The score doesn't just accompany the film; it acts as a geological force within it.

While the pacing sags slightly in the second act during the sietch politics, the convergence of all these massive thematic plates in the finale is breathtaking. A true cinematic spectacle that demands to be seen on the largest screen possible.
